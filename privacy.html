Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

IA Pal Negocio ("the app") is a private automation tool operated by a single individual. It is used only to upload, schedule and manage videos on YouTube channels owned by that same individual. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

Through Google OAuth, with the owner's explicit consent, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and edit their title, description, thumbnail and visibility on the owner's own channels, and to read basic channel and video statistics.

What we collect and store

The app does not collect personal data from visitors or from any other users. The only data stored is the owner's OAuth access/refresh tokens, kept on the owner's own computer, and the owner's own video metadata.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. We do not use it for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
